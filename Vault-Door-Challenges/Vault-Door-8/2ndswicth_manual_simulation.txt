2nd switch bit:

ASCII: 
Ex: 65

Convertion:
65 ÷ 2 = 32 remainder 1
32 ÷ 2 = 16 remainder 0
16 ÷ 2 = 8  remainder 0
8  ÷ 2 = 4  remainder 0
4  ÷ 2 = 2  remainder 0
2  ÷ 2 = 1  remainder 0
1  ÷ 2 = 0  remainder 1

Read from bottom to top:
01000001

=====================================

char mask1 = (char)(1 << p1);
char mask2 = (char)(1 << p2);

____________________

ex:

p1 = 0;
p2 = 3;

1 << 0  --- >   00000001

1 << 3  --- >   00001000
______________________

mask1 = 00000001
mask2 = 00001000

======================================

char bit1 = (char)(c & mask1);
char bit2 = (char)(c & mask2);
